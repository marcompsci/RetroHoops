## Beta App Review / App Review notes ("Notes")

> Retro Hoops is an original arcade basketball game (all teams, players, courts, art and sound are made for the game; no real leagues, teams, players or brands). There's no login of our own and no ads, analytics or tracking. Nearly everything works offline.
>
> **No sign-in needed.** Game Center is optional (Settings ▸ GAME CENTER) and is used for leaderboards and LIVE matchmaking. iCloud save sync is optional (Settings ▸ ICLOUD SYNC).
>
> **Quick look:** PLAY ▸ QUICK CALL ▸ TIP OFF (a single game). THE STREETS (3 on 3 to 21) is on the main menu and in PLAY. BALLER CITY is the walkable city on the main menu. RISE MODE ▸ PLAY NEXT is the story career. 2 PLAYER ▸ one device, two phones nearby, or a Couch Cup.
>
> **In-app purchase:** one non-consumable, **Retro Hoops Pro** ($3.99 US), at PLAY ▸ RETRO HOOPS PRO. It unlocks FRANCHISE mode and Rise seasons 2+, and puts every Hoops Pass cosmetic in the locker (they can also be earned free). Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy are on that screen. LIVE and every other mode are free.
>
> **LIVE (online):** free, through Game Center real-time matchmaking. A match needs a second device signed in to a different Game Center account. We don't run a server for this version. Opponents see each other's Game Center name and a built-in team, never typed text. Chat is **preset quick-chat only** (8 fixed phrases such as "GG!"), and it can be switched off on the LIVE screen. Names that arrive from another phone pass through an on-device word filter. In a LIVE game, the pause menu and end screen have **REPORT PLAYER** (opens an email to the developer with the player's Game Center name and id filled in; the player sends it) and **BLOCK PLAYER** (hides their chat; the game won't match or rematch them again on that device). LIVE ▸ UNBLOCK ALL undoes blocks.
>
> **Two phones nearby** use Multipeer Connectivity (the Local Network prompt appears only when you open 2 PLAYER ▸ TWO PHONES).
>
> **Apple Music (optional):** the in-game phone's RETRO HOOPS playlist is the game's own instrumental beats. MY MIX can play songs the player picks from their own Apple Music via MusicKit (needs their subscription). EXPLICIT SONGS is off by default and iOS Screen Time limits still apply.
>
> **Secret codes:** Settings ▸ SECRETS ▸ ENTER A CODE takes four-symbol codes (hints are earned by playing). They unlock fun toggles (big heads and similar) and hidden teams and courts. They never unlock purchased content.
>
> **Permissions are asked only when used:** Local Network (two phones), add-only Photos (save a highlight), Apple Music (MY MIX). The game works if any is declined.
>

## Beta App Description

> Retro Hoops is an original pixel-art arcade basketball game for iPhone and iPad. Play quick 2-on-2 games, 3-on-3 street ball to 21, a story career, a franchise, a walkable city, two-player games on one phone or two, and free online LIVE matches through Game Center. No ads, no tracking, and nearly everything works offline.
