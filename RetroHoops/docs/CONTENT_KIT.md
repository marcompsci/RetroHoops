# Retro Hoops build-in-public content kit

Platforms (Omari's choice, 8 Oct): **TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X / Threads.**
One clip feeds three video platforms. X and Threads get the text version with a GIF or the clip.
The dated schedule is in `LAUNCH_PLAN.md` § 5; this file holds the ready-to-post words.

## Getting footage

- **Phone (best):** iOS Screen Recording from Control Center while you play the TestFlight build. Portrait with sound. 15–25 seconds.
- **Mac, no hands needed:** double-click `tools/Record Content Clips.command` after a Build Check. It saves six silent portrait clips (two demo games, Full Court, title, CLUTCH, The Park, Locker Room) to `~/RetroHoops/ContentClips/`. Add music in CapCut or the app's editor; use the app's own library sounds, not your own music files, so posts don't get muted.
- **Free stills:** the App Store screenshots in `~/RetroHoops/AppStoreScreenshots/` and the in-game SHARE RESULT card.
- **You on camera:** 2–3 seconds of your face at the start or end makes a "dev" post feel human. Optional, but it helps a lot.

## Rules that keep posts safe

- Only your own game footage. No NBA, team or player names in captions or hashtags.
- Use music from each platform's own library.
- Ask before filming friends, and blur personal info in App Store Connect shots.
- Never say "#1" or quote download numbers you don't have.
