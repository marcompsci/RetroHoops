# Retro Hoops beta: guide for testers

Thanks for helping test **Retro Hoops**, an original pixel-art arcade basketball game for iPhone and iPad. This is a beta: things may break, and that's what we want to find.

## Getting it

1. Install Apple's **TestFlight** app from the App Store.
2. Open the invite link you were sent (or https://testflight.apple.com/join/6F4CqA1R) on your iPhone or iPad and tap **Accept**, then **Install**.
3. Updates show up in TestFlight. Turn on automatic updates there if you like.

Beta builds expire after 90 days. TestFlight tells you when there's a newer one.

## What to try

- **QUICK CALL** (PLAY ▸ QUICK CALL): one game, a few minutes.
- **THE STREETS**: 3 on 3 to 21 with tricks and SHOWSTOPPERS.
- **BALLER CITY**: walk the city, find games and shops.
- **RISE MODE**: the story career.
- **2 PLAYER**: on one device, or two phones next to each other.
- **LIVE**: free online games through Game Center. Sign in to Game Center first (Settings ▸ GAME CENTER).

## Playing online safely

- Opponents in LIVE see your **Game Center name**, never anything you typed in the game.
- Chat is **preset messages only** ("GG!", "NICE SHOT!"…). Turn it off with **QUICK CHAT** on the LIVE screen.
- If someone is abusive, pause (or wait for the end screen) and tap:
  - **BLOCK PLAYER**: their chat is hidden, and the game won't match you with them again on that device. **UNBLOCK ALL** on the LIVE screen undoes it.
  - **REPORT PLAYER**: opens an email to the developer with their Game Center name filled in. Add what happened and send it.

## Purchases in the beta

Retro Hoops Pro is the only purchase (a one-time unlock). In TestFlight, purchases use Apple's test environment, so **you aren't charged**. A purchase in the beta doesn't carry over to the App Store version.

## Sending feedback

- In TestFlight, open Retro Hoops and tap **Send Beta Feedback**, or take a screenshot in the game and tap **Share Beta Feedback**.
- In the game, **SEND FEEDBACK** on the main menu (beta copies only) copies your build number, phone model and game totals so you can paste them into your feedback. Nothing is sent by the game itself.
- Crashes are reported to the developer by TestFlight automatically if you allow it.
- Email: rodlonbell16@gmail.com.

The most useful things to tell us: what you were doing when something went wrong, anything confusing or hard to tap, and whether you'd keep playing.

## Privacy

There are no ads, no tracking and no accounts of our own. Your career stays on your device (and your own iCloud if you turn on iCloud Sync). The full policy: https://github.com/marcompsci/RetroHoops/blob/main/RetroHoops/docs/PRIVACY.md
